# 封号申诉

依据所有者 2026-10-26 提供的经验和申诉模板。申诉由用户本人在官方申诉入口提交（入口见 [M12 的 K4](incidents-and-integrations.md)），Agent 帮用户把模板填好，不代提交。

## 回复时间

**封号申诉的回复时间在 2 到 7 天左右，超过这个时间点基本就凉了。**等回复期间不要重复提交，不要反复登录测试；超过 7 天没回复，按 [A0 第 6、8 条](account-onboarding.md) 修好环境、换邮箱重新注册。

## 解封以后

**解封的号权重确实高，但是盲目跳脸还是死。**解封后继续用原来那套修好的环境，按 [A0 的节奏](account-onboarding.md) 使用：不要一解封就高强度使用、换出口或直接升 Max。

## 申诉模板

表单有两个问题，分别填下面两段。`[ ]` 里的内容自己填写：填官方让你解释的异常行为。按自己的实际情况删改，和实际情况不符的句子删掉。

**What do you use your account for?**

```text
I use my Claude account primarily for legitimate software development and programming-related work, including coding assistance, debugging, technical research, and development tasks. My usage is for personal and professional development purposes, and I do not intentionally engage in any activity that violates Anthropic’s policies or Terms of Service.
```

**Anything else we should know?**

```text
I would like to clarify two important points regarding my account activity.

First, [这里填让你解释的异常行为内容，自己填写]. If your systems recorded any Claude Code activity associated with my account on that date, I would greatly appreciate it if you could carefully review the relevant account activity, login, and access logs, as such activity was not initiated by me.

Second, my account has consistently been accessed and used from a network environment located in a non-restricted region. To the best of my knowledge, I have not intentionally accessed Claude from a restricted or unsupported region.

I take account security and compliance seriously and have always intended to use Claude in accordance with Anthropic’s policies and Terms of Service. I would sincerely appreciate a manual review of my account activity and the circumstances that resulted in the account being placed on hold.

If any additional information or verification is required from me, I am happy to cooperate.

Please review my case and, if possible, restore access to my account.

Thank you for taking the time to review my appeal.
```

祝大家都能顺利解封。
