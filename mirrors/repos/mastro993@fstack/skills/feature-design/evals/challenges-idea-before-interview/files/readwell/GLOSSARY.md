# Glossary

**Title**:
A book as a work, whatever the number of copies the library owns.
_Avoid_: book (when you mean the work), item

**Copy**:
One physical book on the shelf. A title has one or more copies.
_Avoid_: item, unit

**Loan**:
A copy checked out to a member until its due date.
_Avoid_: booking, rental, checkout (as a noun)

**Renewal**:
Moving a loan's due date later while the member keeps the copy.
_Avoid_: extension, rebooking

**Hold**:
A member's place in line for a title whose copies are all out.
_Avoid_: reservation, booking, request

**Overdue**:
A loan whose due date has passed and whose copy isn't back.
_Avoid_: late, expired
