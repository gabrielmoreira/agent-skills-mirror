Today screen for Streaky (iOS habit tracker)
- who: people tracking 3-8 daily habits, open the app 2-4 times a day, usually for under 10 seconds
- job: check off what they did and see what's left today. primary action is marking a habit done
- content: habit name (up to 30 chars), icon, target (8 glasses, 20 min...), current streak, progress for count/timed habits
- states: first day with no habits, all done, a missed day that breaks a streak
- constraints: iOS, one-handed use, Dynamic Type, no ads
- taste: likes Things 3 and the Apple Fitness rings, hates Habitica's gamey clutter
- out of scope: stats/history screens, social stuff
