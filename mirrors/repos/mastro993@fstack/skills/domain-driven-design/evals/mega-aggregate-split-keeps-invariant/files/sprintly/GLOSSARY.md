# Glossary

**Product**:
Something a team builds and plans in sprints.

**Backlog item**:
A piece of work on a product, with a title, a story and an estimate in points.

**Sprint**:
A time box with a capacity in points.

**Commit**:
Plan a backlog item into a sprint.
_Avoid_: assign, schedule

**Release**:
A named, dated delivery of a product.
