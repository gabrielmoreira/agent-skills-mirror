# Direction

Follow this when the look is open (step 3 of [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)).

Hand the direction to the ux-brainstorm skill: invoke it with the brief (what the product is, who uses it, the user's words about the look, any screenshot as a reference) and your step 1 map, scoped to its steps 3 and 4: gather inspiration, then let the user pick a direction from its board. Its pick, with its references, comes back as the direction; the rest of ux-brainstorm (interview, prototypes, handoff) stays out of this run.

A screenshot is a reference: read its palette, radii, spacing rhythm, and type off it, and say which values you estimated.

With the pick question, tell the user what follows it: `DESIGN.md` written from their pick, then the hunt and a proposed system for them to confirm before anything is built.
