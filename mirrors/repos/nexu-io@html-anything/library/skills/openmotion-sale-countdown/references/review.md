# Review checklist

- **Number**: the digits at the end of the roll equal the `--check` output, and that output matches the real time between posting and the sale.
- **Date**: `target.label` says the same moment as `target.date`, including the time zone.
- **Reels**: digits sit centred in their cells with no half-digit visible at rest; the split line is aligned.
- **Text**: no overflow, clipping or overlap at the chosen aspect.
- **Brand**: advertiser name and colour are right; the sign-off is small and on the end card only.
- **Frames**: no blank frames; beats fade cleanly.
