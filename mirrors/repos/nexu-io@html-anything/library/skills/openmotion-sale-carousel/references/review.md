# Review checklist

Take a still in the middle of every product card and one of the end grid.

- **Prices**: each card's regular and sale price and badge match the `--check` output; the strike sits on the regular price.
- **Title**: still true for every product (e.g. every sale price under the amount in the title).
- **Photos**: each card shows its own product, not stretched; the end grid tiles are in the same order.
- **Text**: names, prices and badges do not overflow or overlap the photo at the chosen aspect.
- **Brand**: shop name and colour are right; the sign-off is small and on the end card only.
- **Frames**: no blank frames between cards; the grid is fully visible before the call to action lands.
