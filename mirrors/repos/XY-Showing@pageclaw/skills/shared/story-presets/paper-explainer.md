# Research Paper Explainer PageStory Preset

Preset: `paper-explainer`

## Applies When

Use this preset when showing one or more research papers to an audience for understanding, discussion, or presentation. It can support a talk, an HTML Deck, a scroll-page explainer, or another render target; the target does not define the story.

## Showing Focus

Help the author or presenter show why the work matters, what was investigated or proposed, how the authors approached it, what the evidence supports, where its limits are, and which implications or takeaways deserve emphasis. The audience and presentation context shape the appropriate depth.

## Flexible authoring lens

Potential material includes the audience's starting point, motivating problem, research gap or question, core idea, method, important evidence or results, interpretation, limitations, implications, and memorable takeaways. These are optional lenses, not required headings or a fixed order. A PageStory may foreground a result, build from a problem, compare papers, reconstruct an argument, or use another coherent approach.

Scenes are semantic units, not slides. One scene may synthesize several paper sections, while one difficult idea may need several scenes. Known talk length or delivery mode may influence emphasis and density without determining a slide count.

## Evidence and expression

Distinguish what the paper reports from the presenter's synthesis, critique, analogy, or open question. Preserve the strength and scope of claims, including conditions, baselines, quantities, uncertainty, negative results, and limitations when they matter to the explanation.

Figures, tables, equations, quotations, demos, and other assets may be referenced when they carry useful meaning and are available. PageStory should capture what they demonstrate or support; visual placement and treatment belong to PageCreator.

## Editorial lens

Show the work at a depth and in a sequence appropriate to the audience rather than following the paper's section order. Give background, method detail, and technical notation only when they earn their place. Make complex work accessible without flattening distinctions or overstating novelty, causality, generality, or practical impact.

## Anti-patterns

Do not turn the abstract into a longer abstract, map every paper section to a scene, reproduce every result for completeness, or force a universal problem-method-results-conclusion sequence. Do not hide limitations, invent missing rationale, or present the speaker's interpretation as the authors' established finding.
