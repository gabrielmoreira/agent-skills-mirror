# Creative Portfolio Preset

## Applies When

Use when showing a creator's identity, practice, representative work, and relevant ways to explore or connect.

## Showing Focus

Help the creator show what they make, what they contributed in collaboration, how the work developed, what resulted, and what distinguishes their practice.

## Flexible scaffold

Draw from and reorder identity, selected work, each project's context, role and contribution, process, outcome, and next paths. Projects need not share one case-study structure; omit unsupported parts.

## Important distinctions

Distinguish individual contribution from team outcomes and achieved outcomes from aspirations. Preserve the original wording of client, collaborator, and work status. Treat visuals or links as evidence only when supported by available material.

## Editorial lens

Prioritize a recognizable practice and meaningful differences among projects rather than flattening every work into the same card or metric.

## Anti-patterns

Do not present collaborative outcomes as solo work, invent process, impact, or client endorsement, or create hollow project narratives to satisfy fixed headings.
