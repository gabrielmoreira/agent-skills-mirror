# Scroll Page Target

Support continuous reading, scanning, and interest-driven navigation. Use semantic HTML, a clear heading order, optional navigation, visible focus, and keyboard-accessible interactions. Let the layout follow the content's timeline, relationships, evidence, or cases.

The page must remain readable and zoomable in narrow and wide viewports without horizontal overflow that blocks reading. Do not prescribe one grid or aesthetic. The opening viewport, navigation, columns, information density, and visual language should form a coherent response to the current PageStory rather than follow a fixed page template.

After rendering, inspect wide and narrow viewports for the opening composition, content reflow, clipping, scroll length, and discoverability. Check whether the Hero, images, navigation, whitespace, or other media unexpectedly crowd out primary content. Do not equate source inspection or a passing mechanical detector with visual review.
