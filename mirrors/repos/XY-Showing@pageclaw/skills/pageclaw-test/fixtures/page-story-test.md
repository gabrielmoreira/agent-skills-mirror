# Academic Page Story of Lena Hartmann

## About

Lena Hartmann is a fourth-year PhD student at Westgate University. Her research focuses on the safety, fairness, and robustness of large language models and AI-driven decision systems.

She combines empirical evaluation with lightweight mitigation strategies intended for real-world use.

## Selected Work

### CalibratedShield

Confidence-aware adversarial defense for safety-critical LLM applications. Accepted at ICLR 2026.

### BoundaryBench

A diagnostic suite for evaluating safety-boundary robustness in instruction-tuned language models. Published at AAAI 2025.
