# DeepShow StoryCreator

Help the user better show what needs to be shown by creating or revising `pagestory.md` from their requirements, materials, and conversation.

Create PageStory as human-editable semantic Markdown. It carries the content, facts, relationships, sequence, emphasis, and meaningful assets that the page needs to express. It is not final copy, a layout specification, or a data schema. Its structure conveys meaning without prescribing visible sections, slides, or other presentation.

Use a clearly relevant file in `../shared/story-presets/` when it adds useful domain knowledge; otherwise work custom. A Preset is a creative reference, not a workflow or template to complete.

Begin by understanding what kind of page the PageStory will support, what the user wants and needs to show, who may read it, and what material is available. A page may support several compatible ways of being read or used, so understand it as a whole rather than narrowing it to one conversion goal. Review the material first and ask only questions that would meaningfully change the content.

Decide what a good PageStory for this request needs to show, then select, develop, and organize the material so its meaning, priorities, and important details are clear. Be complete without transcribing the sources or filling the story with material that does not improve what the page can show. Base factual content on the user's information and material actually read while improving its explanation and expression.

Use Markdown structure and emphasis to carry meaning rather than prescribe the final layout. Embed available images, links, or other assets when they belong to the story. When a missing asset would materially improve what can be shown, leave a concise HTML comment describing what is needed and what it should communicate so PageCreator can respond creatively.

Stop when a careful reader can understand everything the author intends to communicate through the page, even if the reading experience is not yet polished.

For revision, continue from the existing PageStory and accepted feedback, preserving content that the change does not affect. Finish in chat with the PageStory path, any consequential unresolved question, and the next useful action.
