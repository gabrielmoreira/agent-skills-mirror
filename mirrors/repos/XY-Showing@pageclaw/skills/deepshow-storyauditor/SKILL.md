# DeepShow StoryAuditor

Review an existing PageStory independently without editing it. Understand what kind of page it supports, what the user wants and needs to show, who may read it, and what material is actually available. Use a clearly relevant Story Preset as creative context, not as a checklist; otherwise review the work as custom. Treat the PageStory's own framing as an editorial proposal rather than a substitute for independent judgment. Ask only when missing context prevents a useful review.

A PageStory is ready when it gives PageCreator a strong semantic basis for helping the user better show what needs to be shown. It does not need to offer a polished reading experience, but its content, emphasis, relationships, and supported detail should be sufficient, and nothing should materially mislead or obscure what matters.

Evaluate it in layers. Its foundation is the content and supported detail without which the page cannot adequately show what it needs to show. Once that foundation is sound, recognize enrichment that adds real depth, distinction, understanding, exploration, or expression. Notice attention debt when irrelevant, repetitive, or disproportionate material makes important content harder to see. Enrichment cannot compensate for a weak foundation, while length and information density are not defects by themselves.

Judge factual fidelity separately from editorial value. Check factual claims against the user's information and material actually available. Treat clearly framed opinion, hypothesis, and creative expression as such while still noticing factual errors inside them. The existence of material does not require its inclusion; an omission matters only when it materially weakens the story. Multi-purpose, non-linear, flat, browsable, sequential, and information-dense PageStories can all be good.

Respond in chat by default. Lead with one clear judgment: continue to PageCreator, return to StoryCreator, or ask the user for information. Preserve effective choices and explain only the smallest set of issues that would materially improve the story. Do not manufacture findings or force a fixed report format.

Give a numerical score or save a review only when the user asks. A score supplements the judgment; it does not replace it or introduce a formula.

Route semantic changes to StoryCreator and presentation-only issues to PageCreator. If the user accepts a suggestion or asks to apply it, read `../deepshow-storycreator/SKILL.md` and revise the existing PageStory under the StoryCreator contract, preserving content the change does not affect.
