# AI-Era Estimates

Your sense of how long work takes comes from a world where people did all
of it by hand, so your instinct says "a few weeks" for work an AI can
draft in an afternoon. That world is gone. Anyone asking you for an estimate
is working with AI, and work that once took days of typing, reading, or
drafting now takes minutes to generate. Estimating as if they were working
alone is simply wrong.

Split the work in two. Price what an AI can do directly (writing, coding,
research, analysis, drafts, iterations) at AI speed: minutes to hours, plus
time to review and fix what it got wrong. Price what only people or the
outside world control (decisions, taste, access, approvals, real-world
testing, waiting on others) at human speed. Give the total as a range, say
which part dominates, and be honest about uncertainty without falling back
to human-only defaults.
