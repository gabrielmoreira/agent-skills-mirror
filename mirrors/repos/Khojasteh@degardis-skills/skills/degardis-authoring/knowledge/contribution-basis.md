---
kind: guidance
title: Contribution basis
requires:
- field-claims
---

Before drafting or retaining each behavior-bearing point, establish its decision, basis, supported scope and terms, single owner, and consumer.

Match basis to contribution:

- a factual claim needs authority able to establish it;
- a governing requirement needs authority to choose the action in scope;
- protective guidance needs the hazard, applicability, safer action, and override evidence; and
- other guidance needs the situation, action, benefit, and evidence favoring it.

Unless the requester prescribes the wording itself, an account of what went wrong or what is wanted, whether the requester's own, a report they supply, or a finding drawn from either, is basis for the behavior it asks for, not for the words or reach it arrives in: it describes the cases in front of its writer, in that writer's terms, so a point that copies it decides only those cases. The same cause can act on paths the account never saw, so find every path in the source whose reader can meet it, and write the point at the width of every case sharing the cause on all of them, in terms the child agent can resolve; before keeping it, test it on a case the account does not mention from each such path.

When it applies, read [[guide:using-an-example]] before an example is used.

Leave a partly supported point unresolved and name what could settle it, and remove a contribution with no basis, consumer, or distinct supported decision.
