kind: guidance
title: Contribution basis
requires:
- field-claims
---

Before drafting or retaining each behavior-bearing point, establish its decision, basis, supported scope and terms, single owner, and consumer.

Match basis to contribution:

- a factual claim needs authority able to establish it;
- a governing requirement needs authority to choose the action in scope;
- protective guidance needs the hazard, applicability, safer action, and override evidence; and
- other guidance needs the situation, action, benefit, and evidence favoring it.

When it applies, read [[guide:using-an-example]] before an example is used.

Leave a partly supported point unresolved and name what could settle it, and remove a contribution with no basis, consumer, or distinct supported decision.
