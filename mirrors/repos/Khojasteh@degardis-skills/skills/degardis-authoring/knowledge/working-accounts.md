kind: guidance
title: Working accounts
---

Keep five accounts distinct in re-readable working state. Initialize each before use, update it before relying on changed state, and stop a dependent step or claim when an entry is missing or stale. If an account is lost, reconstruct it only from inspectable evidence or leave the dependent result open.

1. **Request frame:** outcome, material boundary, change authority, reader, done signal, and host limits.
2. **Decision register:** choices governed by [[principle:decision-provenance]] and their consuming steps.
3. **Coverage:** every member and disposition in each outcome-relevant set, with independently derived sets kept separate until compared.
4. **Evidence:** the question each result settles and remaining gaps.
5. **Workspace:** everything the run created and what must be removed or restored.

The accounts support closing claims; their form is not part of the result.
