A source or artifact is judged in four layers, and no layer stands in for another:

- **Compiler integrity:** what the effective compiler's checks establish.
- **Obligation coverage and static rehearsal:** whether every required decision and terminal state has a supported owner and the generated path derives it.
- **Meaning:** whether the child agent can decide without guessing, including when live statements interact.
- **Composition:** whether each needed provision reaches its consumer before the governed decision.
