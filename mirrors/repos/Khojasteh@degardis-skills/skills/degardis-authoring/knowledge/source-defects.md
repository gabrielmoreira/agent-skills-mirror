---
kind: concept
title: Source defects
---

A **gap** is a required obligation without a supported owner that reaches it before its decision, or without an executable branch; an owner the reader must infer is not supported.

A **contradiction** exists when a reachable actor, under compatible conditions and holding the authority for both, cannot perform both live instructions. A fallback whose trigger excludes an authorized primary action is not one.

An **ownership defect** is a point with no owner, more than one live owner, an owner outside the paths consuming it, or an owner of a construct kind that [[guide:construct-ownership]] rules out for it, even when that owner's text reaches every consumer.

An **ambiguity** exists when the text on a reader's path supports readings that lead to different actions, and nothing on that path decides between them.

Compare whole rules before admitting any of the four. A candidate is ruled out only when the reader on its path, holding only what that path carries, derives a compatible reading; one still open to a conflicting reading is an ambiguity.
