kind: guidance
title: The child agent's working discipline
---

A child agent otherwise supplies its working discipline from habit, along with the principles of its own field. The author's own discipline cannot fill that second-order gap after shipping; [[principle:cold-reader-derivation]] is the test each selected principle must pass in the child skill's generated bundle.

[[guide:principle-selection]] decides the child skill's principle set, [[guide:principle-design]] how a local principle is written and where and when each principle applies, and [[guide:canonical-principle-contract]] governs how each canonical principle is written, changed, or carried; when they apply, read them before that decision is written or accepted.

Each of these guides governs one decision about what the child skill must teach; when one applies, read it before authoring that decision:

- [[guide:child-dependencies]]: the dependency and its fallback;
- [[guide:domain-risk]]: safeguards and compliance claims;
- [[guide:child-reach]]: the readable boundary;
- [[guide:child-authority]]: the authority for each effect;
- [[guide:child-durable-state]]: destination and retention;
- [[guide:child-sensitive-material]]: handling and disclosure; and
- [[guide:child-report-boundary]]: the report boundary.
