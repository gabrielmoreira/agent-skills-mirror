Repair the earliest authored cause. Replace its owning statement and remove obsolete workarounds; adding a correct sentence beside a defective one leaves both live.

Before adding text, inspect the compiled reach of the point's owner. If the provision exists but the child agent misses or misreads it, repair placement or wording rather than create another owner.

Keep unrelated versions, scalar values, and local style stable; style alone is not a defect. Do not cosmetically reflow untouched serialized values. Re-read changed values as the parser will, and never alter a declared format version merely to make an older source look current.
