title: Compiler evidence
---

Discover an operation and read its help before use. Request only the report dimensions the question consumes.

For editable source, establish identity and prefer a non-writing check; build only when the outcome needs an artifact. When they apply, read [[guide:shipped-files]] before deciding on, judging, or packaging shipped files, and [[guide:page-budgets]] before changing placement or content.

Report a finding with its code, the effective compiler's account of its trigger, and named file. Bound conclusions to what that operation establishes and obtain separate evidence for any other question.
