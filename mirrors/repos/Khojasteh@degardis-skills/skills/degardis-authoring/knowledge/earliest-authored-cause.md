title: Earliest authored cause
---

A failure observed in a run, a finding admitted in a review, and a repair share one unit: the **earliest authored cause**, the first authored decision whose absence, ambiguity, reach, order, or missing completion test made the observed family possible. Correcting it removes the whole family without disturbing unrelated behavior; correcting anything later removes one manifestation and leaves the family live.

The child agent's proximate mistake, the sentence nearest the symptom, and the page where it surfaced name manifestations, not the cause. Before deciding whether wording, placement, or another unit is the cause, establish the candidate provision's compiled reach and read it in the assembled page.
