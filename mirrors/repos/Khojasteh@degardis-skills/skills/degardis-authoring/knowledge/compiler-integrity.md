kind: guidance
title: Compiler integrity
requires:
- skill-judgments
---

A warning is a finding. Run a closing check under the strictest policy the effective compiler offers, and treat a warning as barring the compiler-integrity claim until it is resolved, or until the requester or standing instructions accept it and that acceptance is recorded beside the claim; a successful exit dispositions nothing. The warning bars only that claim: where the compiler still builds and composes pages despite it, building, evaluating, and judging those pages go on.
