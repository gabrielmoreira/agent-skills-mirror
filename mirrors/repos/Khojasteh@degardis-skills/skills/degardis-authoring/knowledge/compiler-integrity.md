title: Compiler integrity
requires:
- skill-judgments
---

A warning is a finding. Where the work has Degardis source, supplied or written, run a closing check on its exact final state under the strictest policy the effective compiler offers, and treat a warning as barring the compiler-integrity claim until it is resolved, or until the requester or standing instructions accept it and that acceptance is recorded beside the claim; a successful exit dispositions nothing. A warning bars only that claim: where the compiler still builds and composes pages despite it, building, evaluating, and judging those pages go on. Without such source the compiler has nothing to check, and the work makes no compiler-integrity claim.
