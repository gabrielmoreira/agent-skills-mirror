This page is about what the child skill must teach the child agent.

The canonical [[principle:durable-state]] principle owns whether a record or workspace change is warranted at all. Add subject knowledge only for decisions the principle cannot supply: the authorized destinations, retention contract, restoration method, and evidence that a domain-specific record is required.
