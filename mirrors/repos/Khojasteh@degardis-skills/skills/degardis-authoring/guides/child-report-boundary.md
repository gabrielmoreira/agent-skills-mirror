title: The child agent's report boundary
applicability:
- When the work depends on how the child agent reports the boundary of its work

This page is about what the child skill must teach the child agent.

The canonical [[principle:authority-before-effect]] principle owns the account of what was reached, what was changed, and what stayed outside the boundary. Add subject knowledge only for what the principle cannot supply: the surfaces the child agent names in that account, and where its own boundary falls.
