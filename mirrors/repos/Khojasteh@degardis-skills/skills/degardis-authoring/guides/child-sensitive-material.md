---
title: Sensitive material in the child agent's hands
applicability:
- When the work depends on how the child agent handles material whose disclosure would harm someone
---

This page is about what the child skill must teach the child agent.

The canonical [[principle:sensitive-material]] principle owns the harm test; do not replace it with a closed vocabulary. If the child skill's subject has regulated or confidential classes, teach those as subject knowledge beside the principle; the named classes do not narrow what else can be sensitive.

Establish whether any requested outcome genuinely requires retaining or disclosing such material and what authority can release it. The source may teach a domain-specific sanitized substitute or redaction method, but it cannot treat readability, a familiar destination, or public attribution as permission.
