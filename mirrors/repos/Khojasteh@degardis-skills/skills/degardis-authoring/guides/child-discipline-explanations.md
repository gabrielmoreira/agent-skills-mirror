---
title: Child discipline explanations
applicability:
- Before explaining the working rules a skill must teach its agent
---

Explain the child agent's discipline from what the generated bundle teaches, not the author's habits. [[guide:principle-selection]], [[guide:principle-design]], and [[guide:canonical-principle-contract]] govern the standards it carries. The applicable child guides explain [[guide:child-dependencies]], [[guide:domain-risk]], [[guide:child-reach]], [[guide:child-authority]], [[guide:child-durable-state]], [[guide:child-sensitive-material]], and [[guide:child-report-boundary]].

For each such decision, explain what the child agent can derive from its generated pages, request, and host contract. Author-side safeguards do not teach the child agent a safeguard: the answer must identify the instruction that reaches it before the governed action, or leave that teaching unestablished.
