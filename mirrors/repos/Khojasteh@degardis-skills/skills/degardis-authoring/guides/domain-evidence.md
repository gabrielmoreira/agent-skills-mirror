---
title: Domain evidence and freshness
applicability:
- When the work depends on a field claim that supplied evidence does not establish
---

Establish each needed proposition from an authority capable of settling that proposition, at the scope in which the skill will use it. The right authority depends on the question: a requester's own policy can govern their organization; an official specification or first-party documentation can establish a product contract; a regulator, statute, or controlling standard can establish an external rule; and observed tests can establish behavior of the tested system and configuration.

Record the qualifiers that can change the decision — version, date, jurisdiction, product/configuration, audience, and whether a statement is normative, advisory, or observed.

Choose explicitly between a **snapshot** and **runtime retrieval**. Embed stable knowledge the child agent must have when its authoritative scope is clear. For volatile knowledge, teach the child agent to retrieve from a named authoritative source only when that source and access method will actually be available and authorized at runtime; define the freshness/version check and the stop or fallback when retrieval fails. If neither an acceptable snapshot nor a reachable runtime authority exists, the dependent behavior remains blocked or is narrowed under authority.

A citation is maintenance provenance, not executable knowledge. The child agent must receive the decision-relevant substance it needs or a reliable procedure to obtain it.
