---
title: Restructuring on evidence
applicability:
- When the work depends on whether a source's present arrangement is causing a problem
---

Restructure when evidence shows the present arrangement is causing the problem: copies that have diverged, one decision with several owners, a point held by a construct kind that [[guide:construct-ownership]] rules out for it, conditional material that is always loaded, a unit whose readers come to it for different work, often shown by a long condition or cue list, the same workaround appearing repeatedly, or no single current unit that can own the repair coherently. Record those signs before choosing a new arrangement, then choose the smallest arrangement that removes the cause while preserving meaning.

"Cleaner" is not a reason, and a move that changes nothing for the child agent buys nothing while still risking what every move risks.

A move is worth making when it changes reach, removes duplicate load or material for work its reader is not doing, creates an independently reusable unit, or makes a decision materially clearer. Judge its execution effect by behavioral preservation, contribution by contribution, using generated reach and page cost as evidence about what readers receive rather than as the definition of behavior.
