---
title: Construct agreements
applicability:
- When the work depends on whether a source's constructs agree with one another
---

Treat agreements among constructs as their own surface, kept in re-readable working state. Preservation does not establish that the final source agrees with itself. Generate this surface wherever one construct's correctness depends on another, including every field or kind exposed by the effective format, and at least:

- description against every task request class;
- stance against every task goal and against the pages that already own its points, with changes to description, stance, goals, or cues checked against the same agreements;
- task cues against one another and against [[guide:task-cues]], each outcome's intended task against the compiled first-match winner, and each compound request's intended order against the compiled order;
- each hand-off condition against the situations its sending task can meet, against the cues, and against [[guide:handoff-conditions]];
- each unit's stated scope against every task page carrying it;
- every point against its single owner, and every unit against the work each reader reaches it for;
- guide and principle applicability against every run that needs the body, on every path that references the page, and against naming a task or route;
- facet index boundaries against domain evidence, overlap composition, and the removal, addition, and compensating-prose tests in [[guide:facet-design]];
- each title against the body it identifies, names and headings against [[guide:names-and-headings]], prose references against [[guide:wording-and-references]], and each guide against [[guide:guide-design]];
- selected principles against the child skill's work, and their placement, applicability, and any local principle's content against [[guide:principle-design]];
- generated page sizes against the effective compiler's budgets; and
- every other dependency between constructs that the effective format exposes.

Admit a contradiction only where its definition holds.

For every guide or principle with applicability, check both sides of reach: when it applies, every execution needing the body reaches it before the governed decision; when it does not, executions do not inherit obligations from it, and later evidence making it apply must bring the page into reach before dependent work continues.

A title and body disagree when the title promises a decision the body does not provide.

Give every agreement a held, ruled-out, or unreachable disposition. Report deviations and material limits rather than copying the working inventory into the result.
