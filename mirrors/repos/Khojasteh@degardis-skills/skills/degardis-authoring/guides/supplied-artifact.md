title: Supplied artifact
applicability:
- When the work depends on a skill in built form rather than its editable source
---

## Stable identity

Establish one effective root and the identity of everything under it as [[guide:artifact-identity]] defines it. When an artifact cannot be held stable while the work runs, use a private copy only when that destination, persistence, and content are authorized, preserving every element the identity covers. Otherwise do not create state merely to stabilize the evidence; the identity is then unavailable.

## Reading the artifact

Take the artifact's layout, section names, and reference conventions from what it itself shows, and each reference's identity from its link target rather than its link text. It is evidence rather than a workspace, so do not change it, execute bundled code in order to inspect it, follow a link outside the effective root, or extract an entry whose path escapes that root.

## Attribution limits

Judge routing, instructions, reachable references, shipped files, and observable behavior. Authored organization, omitted material, history, and where a defect originated were never put into generated output, so a finding may not reach them from an artifact alone. Name each of those as unsupported without matching source, rather than leaving a report that reads as though the artifact had settled them.
