---
title: Behavioral preservation
applicability:
- When the work depends on whether behavior affected by a source change survives or must survive that change
---

Behavior belongs to the decisions the child agent can derive, not to the source form carrying them. Content, headings, conditions, boundaries, references, and whole constructs may change while their execution effect survives; unchanged page membership establishes reach, not equivalence.

Keep a preservation account in re-readable working state whose unit is each behavior-bearing contribution the change can affect — discriminator, action, threshold, fallback, verification, guide contract, or example carrying decision logic — rather than its container. Close each once as carried, equivalently rewritten, merged, split, re-homed, demonstrably generalized, withdrawn under named authority, or unresolved. Report every unresolved contribution. Carry an unestablished claim the request does not authorize correcting, and resolve conflicting starting contributions into one decision rather than move the conflict to runtime.

Preservation looks backward and cannot detect inconsistency introduced by the change, so closing a change covers every agreement touched by an added, removed, rewritten, or re-homed construct as well as every starting contribution.
