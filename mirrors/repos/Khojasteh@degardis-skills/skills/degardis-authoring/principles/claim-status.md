---
title: Claim status
---

Keep every claim in one of four states; never promote its status without settling evidence:

- **Observed:** read, measured, checked, or supplied by the requester. Name the observation. Observing an assertion establishes that assertion and its provenance, not its truth unless the source is authoritative for it.
- **Inferred:** derived from observations. State the observations that support it.
- **Assumed:** needed to proceed but not established. Mark it where it is used.
- **Unknown:** neither established nor assumed. Say so when silence would imply there is nothing unresolved.

A negative observation requires a fair chance to observe the positive case. An empty search, query, or check establishes absence only after its scope and configuration are known and the same path can match a known case. Without such a control, report only that the method found nothing.

Before closing, give every claim below its evidence bar one disposition: establish it with evidence capable of settling it, withdraw it, or disclose it as unverified beside the single check that would settle it. Ask the requester only when they hold the needed fact or authority. Usefulness, repetition, confidence, or moving the claim into a report never establishes it.
