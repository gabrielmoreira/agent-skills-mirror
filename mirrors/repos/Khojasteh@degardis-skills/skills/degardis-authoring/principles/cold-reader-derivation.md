---
title: Derivation by a cold reader
applicability:
- Before concluding what a source or bundle teaches
---

The author reads its own artifact with everything it knows, so the reading that feels complete is the least reliable evidence of what the artifact teaches: a decision the author supplies without noticing reads as taught and ships as a gap.

Authoring has two agents in view: this run, and the **child agent** that will execute the **child skill**, the skill whose source or artifact this run's work concerns. A **first-order** provision governs this run: what it may read, how it establishes claims, or what it reports. A **second-order** provision governs the child agent: it tells this run what the child skill must teach. References to *the child skill*, its source, or *the child agent*, and instructions about designing the child skill, mark the second kind.

Whatever field the child skill serves, a provision is taught only when the child agent, reading cold, derives the required decision from the generated bundle together with the request and the host contract. Everything else this run holds counts only where the bundle, the request, or the host exposes it to the child agent: the authored files as this run arranged them, its session, the manual it read, its conversation with the requester, its own host and capabilities, and the discipline it practices. Author behavior never satisfies a second-order provision; ask the proof question of each one explicitly: from the final generated bundle, can the child agent derive this?

Judge the generated page, never the authored file. Composition decides what reaches the reader and in what order, so a relevant sentence somewhere in the authored material is not reach: a provision that arrives after its decision, sits on a page the reader's path does not open, or waits behind applicability that will not hold is not taught.

A provision the child agent cannot derive stays open. Confidence, familiarity with the subject, and this run's own competence at the discipline are not clearance; report the gap with the step the reader would have to supply. A brief to a delegate and a handoff to a later session are governed by their own principles; this one governs the artifact.
