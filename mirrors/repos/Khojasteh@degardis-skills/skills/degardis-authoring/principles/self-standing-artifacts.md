---
title: Self-standing artifacts
applicability:
- Before writing anything that will be used without the process that produced it
---

An artifact's reader holds neither your process nor your context. Whatever the artifact says about how it came to be reads as part of what it is: a rejected option reads as a constraint, as authority to act, or as a question still open, and what holds is buried among it. Unless the artifact exists to record that history, write it as what holds. Do not define it against an earlier version, an option weighed or rejected, the conversation that shaped it, or an assumption only the process held.

Nor does the reader stand where you stood. What you could consult, reach, or use while producing the artifact, and the names and conventions of the setting you worked in, may not exist where it is used, and who uses it, and where, can change after you deliver it. A reference to anything its reader is not established to have, such as a document, a person, a place, or a name used only where you worked, sends them nowhere, however plainly it names its target. So everything the artifact relies on is either carried in it or stated as a prerequisite the reader can check without access to your setting, named by what is needed rather than by where you found it: *the current price list*, not *the one pinned up in the back office*. Where the reader's setting is unknown, assume none of the possible ones: write for what they share, and state as a prerequisite each assumption the artifact cannot avoid.

The occasion that produced the artifact, whatever prompted it and whatever was in front of you while you made it, shows its purpose through one use, in the terms of whoever brought it, and unless the artifact exists for that use alone, its readers bring uses the occasion never showed. Whatever the occasion decides for such an artifact fits that use alone, such as a name that says why the thing was made rather than what it is, a statement that covers the cases shown rather than every case sharing their reason, parts shaped for that use and placed where the work happened to be rather than where their roles belong, or a standard its kind is held to set aside because this use did not call for it. Take each such decision from what the artifact is for and the whole range of its uses instead, treating the occasion as one of them. Reading the result as its reader would does not catch the difference, since an artifact fitted to one use reads as complete to anyone holding only it.

State a boundary in the artifact only where a reader not told of it would act across it or take what lies beyond it as covered. Write it as part of what holds: the extent of what the artifact covers or permits, and, where something beyond it is still open, who decides it. That an option was considered, refused, or ruled out is process however it is marked, and naming a boundary the reader would never approach introduces the option it excludes.

Reasoning about what holds, such as why a rule is right, belongs in the artifact when its purpose includes reasoning. Reasoning about the change is process, not evidence to that reader: what the artifact said before, why it changed, and what was weighed and dropped. Put each only in a record or report whose purpose includes it, such as a change record for what changed and why or a decision record for the options weighed and each one's status, and never beside the changed value merely because it explains the change. A superseded or rejected option establishes nothing about what holds, so it appears only in such a record or report, marked there with its status.

Before delivering the artifact, read it as its reader would, holding only the artifact and what that reader is established to have. Remove each statement that changes nothing they would do or take to hold about its subject, and resolve each reference they could not follow, by carrying what they need from it or stating it as a prerequisite. A statement about the artifact itself rather than its subject, such as its intended reader or its progress, stays only when its reader acts on it, as on a prerequisite to meet before using it or a status saying whether it is still in force; otherwise it reports on the work that produced it and belongs in the report to whoever asked for that work. Last, test it against a use it must serve that differs from its occasion in exactly what it claims to cover, rather than one drawn from inside the occasion's frame, and correct whatever that use would need decided differently.
