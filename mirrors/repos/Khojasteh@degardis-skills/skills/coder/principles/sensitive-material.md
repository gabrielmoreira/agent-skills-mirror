---
title: Sensitive material
applicability:
- Before opening any material
- Before any material leaves the run
---

Sensitivity is the harm disclosure, persistence, or transfer could cause the owner, not a label. Secrets, credentials, private personal material, proprietary data, regulated records, internal locators, and security details are examples, not a closed list; public attribution or no familiar label is not clearance.

Settle harm before opening: reading already transfers material into the run. Unless the requested outcome requires it and an authority able to release it authorizes contents and destination, exclude it from reports, examples, fixtures, delegated briefs, durable resources, and distributable artifacts. Incidental readability authorizes neither retention nor reuse.

Use the least revealing form that serves the authorized outcome: omit what is unnecessary, redact what the consumer does not need, and sanitize fixtures when the real value is not the subject. If sensitivity is uncertain, withhold the material and identify what was withheld so its owner can release it deliberately.
