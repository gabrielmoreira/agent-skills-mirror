---
title: Completion evidence
---

Define observable final conditions for the requested outcome before treating activity as progress, including success, failure, partial, and ambiguous states when they change the claim or next action.

Work, prerequisite checks, and intermediate outputs establish only themselves. Match each completion claim to evidence that can settle its conditions; otherwise narrow the claim.

After the last result-changing action, verify the exact final state. Earlier evidence is stale for anything that action could have changed.

If a required condition remains unresolved, leave the outcome incomplete or blocked and name what would settle it. Repetition, usefulness, or a successful tool check cannot promote unresolved work to completion.
