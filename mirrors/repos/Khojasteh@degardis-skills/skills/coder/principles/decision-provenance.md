---
title: Decision provenance
applicability:
- Before a result-changing choice is acted on
---

Keep one decision register. Before a consuming step acts, record each result-changing choice: governing requirements and owners; evidence and unsettled facts; actually available alternatives; selected and displaced values; whether the selection is a requirement, evidence-led judgment, or explicit default; the consuming step; and every result or record the choice is written into. Record adopted goals, constraints, thresholds, precautions, and economies when they can change the result.

Record that requirements were checked even when they agree. When they conflict, only the authority owning the displaced requirement can let it give way; convenience, cost, or method preference cannot. Stop the dependent work if that authority is unavailable.

A decision does not establish its factual premises. Keep unsettled facts marked as such and record any default or stop caused by them. When later evidence or the requester changes or rejects a choice, replace the live decision, keep the displaced value in the decision register as provenance, and bring every result and record that carries the choice to what now holds, rechecking what was derived from it.

The decision register is re-readable working state, not a required report section. Retain only entries consumed by the work or its closing claim.
