---
title: Defect repair
applicability:
- When a change corrects a software defect
---

Before a production edit, connect the established expected behavior, reproduced symptom, first causal divergence, correction, invariant to restore, and evidence that will prove the correction. Identify the production and verification surfaces likely to change, behavior deliberately left unchanged, and applicable compatibility, state, security, rollout, and documentation work. Revise this plan when implementation evidence disproves an assumption or expands the causal boundary. A file checklist, speculative cleanup, redesign, migration, or dependency work does not establish a repair. Edit only once this evidence and readiness path is complete, and stop if new evidence invalidates the diagnosis or the plan.

Prefer independent executable evidence at the smallest real failure boundary, observed failing for the demonstrated reason before the correction as [[principle:software-evidence]] requires. Decide separately whether that evidence belongs in the permanent suite: update an existing test that already owns the contract, retain new coverage when it protects durable behavior or a realistic recurrence mechanism, and use a temporary one-shot check when permanent maintenance cost is not justified. Use higher-level retained coverage when wiring, serialization, persistence, lifecycle, concurrency, or integration caused the defect. Low retention value never excuses missing before-and-after evidence.

Use non-test evidence only after demonstrating that an automated failing check is unsafe, destructive, environment-bound, nondeterministic, disproportionately impractical, or outside what the work may run, and that no inner layer reproduces the mechanism. Substitute deterministic causal evidence such as a trace, crash artifact, invariant violation, isolated reproducer, or protocol transcript; state its remaining uncertainty and how the correction will be verified. Inspection alone is not proof when safe reproduction is available to the work.

Repair the defective guarantee rather than the change that exposed it. Retries, sleeps, null guards, broad catches, defaults, or resets that merely hide the symptom are not a correction. Choose the simplest safe causal correction by correctness, compatibility, regression surface, fit with established architecture, and total implementation, maintenance, operational, and future-change cost—not edited-line count. Preserve established architecture, versions, tooling, interfaces, and unrelated behavior. Do not special-case only the reported example when the cause reaches a broader valid domain, mix in broad refactoring, or add opportunistic renaming, extraction, formatting, upgrades, or abstractions.

Verify by rerunning the causal evidence first and showing it passes because the demonstrated mechanism changed, before the focused and broader adopted checks the combined change warrants. Also verify affected failure timing, side effects, cleanup, compatibility, and nearby behavior. A compilation, green broad suite, or symptom not observed once does not establish a fix. State when only an equivalent mechanism was verified, which material check was skipped or unavailable, and when no retained automated guard remains and why.
