---
title: Failure investigation
applicability:
- When the cause of an observed or reported failure must be established
---

Define reproduction before attempting it: exact input, relevant state, environment, timing, command, output, frequency, observed result, expected result, what counts as reproduced, and the attempt or time bound. Establish a focused baseline and distinguish pre-existing failures. Prefer the innermost safe layer that can exhibit the symptom without an unavailable device, service, environment, or privilege; do not call a failure environment-bound while an inner serialization, query construction, command construction, encoding, arithmetic, or other causal layer remains testable. Minimize external effects, use bounded disposable resources, and obtain authority for a destructive, privileged, spend-incurring, or externally visible attempt. Do not encode the suspected cause in a substitute or mock, treat one flaky result as a mechanism, or repeat an equivalent failed attempt.

Start the causal search at the first failing layer, classified under [[principle:software-evidence]]: a failure that the test, harness, environment, or configuration already explains is diagnosed there rather than in production code.

Trace from the trigger to the first incorrect state, decision, or missing guarantee, then onward to every relevant visible impact. Form one causal hypothesis, verify uncertain API, framework, concurrency, lifecycle, serialization, and persistence semantics, test the hypothesis against the reproduction and strongest competing explanation, and revise it when evidence disagrees. Consolidate symptoms only when one cause explains them. A correlated change or failing line is not yet the cause, and a change that exposed an older latent defect is distinct from one that introduced it: the cause is the defective guarantee, not the change that exposed it.

For intermittent behavior, first use existing logs, traces, crash artifacts, telemetry, and job history to compare failing and successful occasions. Common, non-exhaustive mechanism classes are ordering or timing, uninitialized or shared mutable state, resource exhaustion or leaks, cancellation or cleanup, input or data variance, environment or configuration variance, and external dependency behavior. Rank only mechanisms the evidence admits, then make one observable or forceable by controlling a clock, seed, replay, interleaving, resource, dependency, or bounded instrumentation rather than repeating the original attempt. Vary one evidence-led dimension at a time and preserve the frequency and conditions of each result. Source instrumentation requires change authority and remains temporary unless the requested outcome adopts it. For an unreproduced report, stop at the fixed bound and report what the attempts establish, what they do not, and the next discriminating observation; disappearance of the symptom is not a fix.

To locate a regression, first establish a known-good and known-bad revision under the same decisive reproduction. Test candidate revisions in an isolated checkout that preserves the active workspace, record the revision, environment, command, and verdict for each candidate in re-readable working state, and stop when the boundary is narrow enough to inspect causally. The boundary identifies a change to investigate; distinguish a defect introduced there from an older defect merely exposed by that change.
