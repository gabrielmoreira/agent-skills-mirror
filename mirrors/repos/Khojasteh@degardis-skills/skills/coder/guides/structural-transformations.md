---
title: Structural transformation safeguards
applicability:
- When a structural transformation is meant to preserve observable behavior
---

Establish the observable contract and a preservation baseline before editing. A characterization test is valid only for behavior established as intended.

Use design heuristics to generate and challenge candidate target structures before selecting a transformation. Choose the smallest transformation that removes the cost: extract a coherent operation, separate independently varying responsibilities, move behavior to the owner of its knowledge, collapse an abstraction with no remaining purpose, group a real value concept, rename a misleading symbol, clarify conditional structure, separate interleaved phases, centralize mutation, consolidate one behavior, introduce a narrow evidence seam, or delete proven unreachable code. An unlisted transformation is acceptable when its precondition, preservation contract, and sensitive check are stated. Compare viable transformations by implementation risk and total implementation, maintenance, operational, and future-change cost; the smallest diff is not necessarily the lowest-cost result.

For each transformation, state the structural cost it removes, the precondition that makes the operation valid, the observable behavior and compatibility it must preserve, the evidence sensitive to that behavior, and the signal that would make the operation the wrong choice. Common, non-exhaustive safeguards include:

- **Extract an operation** only when its inputs, outputs, effects, failure behavior, and name form one meaningful operation or decision whose name clarifies the caller. Preserve evaluation order, captures, mutation, side effects, exceptions, and asynchronous behavior; read the caller afterward and reject an extraction that did not improve it, needs control flags or an incoherent parameter list, or creates a navigation chain of one-line operations.
- **Split a class or unit** only around a cohesive concept whose responsibilities vary independently or obscure the original purpose. Define ownership, lifetime, visibility, and communication, and verify construction, serialization, dependency injection, cleanup, and public behavior. Reject a data-and-delegation shell or a split whose parts must still be understood and changed together.
- **Move responsibility** only toward the cohesive owner of the knowledge or state it uses, where the current placement creates knowledge leakage, repeated policy, or an unstable dependency direction. Preserve call order, visibility, ownership, lifetime, transaction, and serialization boundaries; update every caller and reject a move that merely satisfies a layer diagram, reverses a dependency, creates a cycle, or accumulates unrelated responsibilities.
- **Inline indirection** only after establishing that it owns no independent policy, variation, reuse, test seam, or compatibility purpose. Close callers, overrides, registrations, doubles, and dynamic references; preserve evaluation, errors, lifetime, visibility, and every real trust, I/O, process, platform, or volatility boundary.
- **Group parameters** only when they have a stable relationship, shared validation, or coordinated evolution and therefore form one value concept. Preserve defaults, nullability, ordering, overload behavior, serialization, ownership, call compatibility, and external construction or binding paths; shortening a signature is not enough.
- **Rename a symbol** only when its current name misstates established domain meaning. Use language-aware tooling where reliable and search textual, generated, reflective, configuration, template, route, serialization, database, and external references. Preserve public, wire, configuration, database, and compatibility names unless their change is separately authorized, and do not combine a rename with logic, signature, ownership, or formatting changes.
- **Rewrite a condition** only when its structure obscures cases, precedence, or domain meaning. Preserve evaluation order, short-circuiting, repeated evaluation, side effects, null handling, exceptions, precedence, overloaded or non-boolean operand behavior, and the closed or open nature of the cases. Check truth-table boundaries and do not give a side-effecting predicate a name that conceals the effect.
- **Split phases** only when interleaved acquisition, parsing, validation, decision, or effect responsibilities obscure failures, state transitions, or independent verification. Define data and error contracts between phases; preserve ordering, transactions, cleanup, retries, cancellation, partial failure, and atomicity; reject pass-through phases with no independent policy or meaning.
- **Centralize mutation** only when scattered mutation or temporary invariant violations can be put under one owner. Define valid transitions, ownership, synchronization, transaction, persistence, ordering, notification, error, serialization, and concurrency behavior, then close every mutation path. Accessors that leave the same mutation surface, stale copies, contention, or a longer-lived global owner do not establish encapsulation.
- **Consolidate fragments** only when one cohesive behavior requires unnecessary navigation across units that do not vary independently. Preserve meaningful trust, I/O, process, lifetime, and compatibility boundaries; remove obsolete forwarding, registrations, interfaces, and construction wiring; and confirm navigation fell without creating a god object.
- **Introduce a test seam** only for specific evidence blocked by time, randomness, I/O, environment, global state, construction, concurrency, or an external system. Put that dependency under caller control at its real boundary, preferring a direct parameter, extracted pure decision, boundary adapter, or established platform seam over a new framework or interface hierarchy, and verify production wiring, ownership, and lifetime.
- **Remove dead code** only after establishing that it is obsolete or that every supported entry path is unreachable. Use the complete consumer search and affected build or package evidence rather than no static references, no recent execution, or a test that merely asserts absence; check supported versions before deleting compatibility paths and remove registrations, tests, documentation, and configuration made obsolete by the deletion.

Apply one conceptual transformation at a time and verify the combined result at the boundary consumers observe. Confirm the cost fell rather than moved and that the replacement adds no larger burden. Do not report a transformation as an improvement when that comparison fails; an authorized implementation revises or safely reverts its own separable change. If every useful transformation changes behavior, stop treating the request as restructuring.

Keep intermediate states buildable and reviewable when practical, inspect the diff of each unit, and keep the units distinguishable in a plan or review.
