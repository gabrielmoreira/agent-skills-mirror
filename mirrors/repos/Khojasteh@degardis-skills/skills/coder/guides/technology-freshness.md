---
title: Current technology state
applicability:
- When a decision depends on the state of an external technology or service as of a particular time
---

Resolve the time-relative term before using it to make a decision. Establish what subject it applies to, the relevant release channel, edition, platform, or other scope, and the point in time the request requires. Keep that state distinct from versions or configurations already present in the project.

Use an authority capable of establishing the requested state. For product releases and availability, use the product owner or its authoritative distribution channel; for support and deprecation, use the governing support policy or documentation; for standards and external contracts, use the authority that controls them. Preserve the version, date, channel, platform, and other qualifiers that determine what the evidence establishes.

Use the established current state only for decisions that require it. Do not replace the project's resolved state, select an upgrade, change compatibility targets, or adopt a newly available mechanism unless the requested outcome or another governing requirement calls for that change.

When several authoritative sources describe different scopes or effective dates, keep those distinctions rather than combining them into one state. When the required current state cannot be established from an authorized and reachable authority, leave the dependent decision unresolved and continue work that does not require it.

Stop once the time-relative fact needed by the work is established. Later, broader, or adjacent technology information does not increase the authority of that result.
