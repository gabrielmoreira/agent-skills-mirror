# Brainstorming Planning Checklist

- User and use case are specific.
- Problem is distinct from proposed solution.
- Success criteria are observable.
- Constraints and non-goals are explicit.
- Questions are limited to material unknowns; supplied decisions are reused.
- At least two alternatives were considered.
- MVP avoids solving speculative future needs.
- Risks and unknowns have validation steps.
- Final brief is detailed enough to plan implementation without re-litigating intent.
- Authorized implementation continues after shaping; planning-only requests
  stop at the requested brief.
