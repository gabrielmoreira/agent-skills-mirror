# Review

Make a contact sheet (one frame every 0.5–1 s) and full-size key frames at each scene's midpoint, then
tick each line and report it.

## Style
- [ ] A viewer who knows the style would name it from any single mid-scene frame.
- [ ] Only card colours (plus `brandColors`) appear; sample two or three frames to check.
- [ ] The card typefaces rendered (no fallback serif or default sans); Chinese uses the `cjk` face.
- [ ] At least two rules are visible and labelled correctly; no item from the card's `avoid` list appears.
- [ ] Motion uses the card's moves; nothing is carried by fades alone.

## Text
- [ ] No overflow, clipping or overlap, including mid-animation; text inside a colour block keeps clear padding to its edge.
- [ ] Every reading line, rule plates included, is at least 30 px at 1080p and stays long enough to read (`line_unread` is clear).
- [ ] Headlines match `plan.json` exactly; no invented numbers, quotes or dates.

## Brand
- [ ] OpenMotion wordmark visible in the opening; "Made with OpenMotion" held at least 1.5 s at the end.
- [ ] The user's own logo is unmodified.

## Timing
- [ ] No blank or black frames; entrances land on the beat grid.
- [ ] Length matches the plan within one frame.

Say which checks came from still frames and what the user still has to watch or hear in motion.
