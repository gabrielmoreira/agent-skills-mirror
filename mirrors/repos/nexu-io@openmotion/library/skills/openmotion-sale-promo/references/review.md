# Review checklist

Make a contact sheet (one frame every 0.5 s) and stills at the middle of each beat.

- **Numbers**: the regular price, sale price, percentage and saving on screen match the `--check` output exactly; the strike line sits on the regular price and draws before the sale price moves.
- **Code and deadline**: spelled exactly as given, held still for at least 1.5 s, deadline matches the shop's own wording.
- **Photo**: the right product, not stretched, nothing important cropped by the card.
- **Text**: no overflow, clipping or overlap at the chosen aspect; translated labels fit.
- **Brand**: advertiser name and colour are right; the OpenMotion sign-off is small and on the end card only.
- **Frames**: no blank or black frames; every beat fades cleanly into the next.
