# Attributions

## Sources

This skill's persona framework draws on the following external work:

- **[conversation-design-library-v2](https://github.com/phil-haenggi/conversation-design-library-v2)** — licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), used with attribution.

- **Deibel, D. & Evanhoe, R., *Conversations with Things* (Rosenfeld Media)** — a source for the skill's conversational-design and personification concepts.

### Research & Industry Guidelines

- **[Conversation Design Institute (CDI)](https://conversationdesigninstitute.com/)** — Foundational principles on intentional persona design, including the pareidolia effect, an emphasis on natural and consistent responses, identity, and register.

- **[Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tone-of-voice-dimensions/)** — Research on voice and tone in UX writing, the distinction between voice (persistent) and tone (contextual), and usability heuristics that inform dimension boundaries. Kate Moran, "The Four Dimensions of Tone of Voice," updated 2023.

- **Peter, S., Riemer, K., & West, J. D., ["The benefits and dangers of anthropomorphic conversational agents"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12146756/)** — Research on user perceptions of conversational agents.

### Platform Design Guidelines

- **[Amazon Alexa Design Guidelines](https://developer.amazon.com/en-US/docs/alexa/alexa-design/get-started.html)** — Voice channel parameters (pitch, speaking rate, energy) and voice-specific persona considerations.

- **[Google Conversation Design Guidelines](https://developers.google.com/assistant/conversation-design)** — Principles for persona definition, error handling patterns, and turn-taking in conversational interfaces.

- **[How to Speak AI: A Practical Guide to Writing Effective Prompts](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/speak-ai-prompts/)**

- **[Should You Give Your AI Agent a Human Name?](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/naming-an-ai-agent/)**
