    9–15 min · Define the terms

    Three levels, three questions

    LEVEL 1

    Inventory

    What exists?
    • Owners and creators
    • Users and segments
    • Access and permissions
    • Risk signals
    Registry · identity · lifecycle
    LEVEL 2

    Observability

    How does each one behave?
    • Calls, latency, errors
    • Quality scores
    • Cost and consumption
    • Regressions between versions
    Traces · metrics · evaluations
    LEVEL 3

    Population mining

    What patterns emerge overall?
    • Correlations over time
    • Comparisons between segments
    • Drift, duplication, virality
    • Invisible one unit at a time
    History · correlation · analytics
    We move from observing one unit to intelligence about the population.

    Level 1 answers "what", level 2 answers "how", level 3 looks for patterns that are invisible one unit at a time.
