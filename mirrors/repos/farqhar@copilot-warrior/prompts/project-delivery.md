# Prompts for project delivery and field teams

For people who make decisions standing in front of a drawing, not sitting in a Teams meeting.

---

### Turn a site walk or in-person meeting into decisions and actions

**App:** Copilot Notebooks (Capture) · **Role:** Project / field · **Licence:** Microsoft 365 Copilot · **Last verified:** pending re-test

**Prompt:**
Tell everyone you're recording, then use **Capture** in Copilot Notebooks (on iPhone and iPad it's in the OneNote app, under Copilot Notebooks) to record the conversation and take a few photos. Afterwards, ask the notebook:
> From today's [site walk / meeting], list every decision we made, every item someone agreed to action with their name, and anything raised that needs a formal follow-up (a query, a change or an instruction). Flag any safety items separately.

**Use when:** the real decisions happened on a walk-through, a toolbox talk or around a whiteboard, so they never reach the meeting minutes.

**Where it falls short:** you are responsible for getting everyone's consent before you record. Treat that as a hard rule, not a formality. Wind, plant noise and people talking over each other all degrade the transcript. Capture only works with work or school accounts, it arrived on iPhone and iPad first, and some Windows versions were still in Insiders Beta at launch. Names are the weakest part. Check every action owner.

### Point a Notebook at public web pages and your docs, then find the gaps

**App:** Copilot Notebooks · **Role:** Project / field · **Licence:** Microsoft 365 Copilot · **Last verified:** pending re-test

**Prompt:**
In your project notebook, choose **Add references → Link** and paste the URL of each public requirement page (a regulator's guidance, a standard's summary, a client's published requirements). Add your own plan or procedure as a file reference. Then ask:
> Using the web pages I've added and the project documents, list every requirement that applies to [project or activity]. For each one, say whether our [plan / procedure] covers it, and quote the line that covers it. Flag anything it doesn't cover.

**Use when:** you need to check your plan against external requirements, and the requirements live on someone else's website.

**Where it falls short:** links must be publicly reachable. Anything behind a login or paywall won't load. Copilot reads the page as it is *today*, so if the rules change, re-run the check. "Covered" is Copilot's judgement. Asking for the quoted line lets you check that judgement in seconds. This is not a compliance sign-off.
