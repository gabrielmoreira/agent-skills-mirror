# Skill: Technical SEO Audit

## Purpose
Coordinate reproducible technical findings across crawl, indexability, configuration,
redirects and structured data.

## Procedure
Run deterministic rules; group findings by template and affected URL; attach crawl evidence;
rank by impact, confidence and effort; produce remediation and verification plans.

## Guardrails
Do not claim direct crawl-budget control or search-engine internals. Do not present a
synthetic score as a Google metric.
